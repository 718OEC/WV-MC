Barter Bazaar

Rules:
1. No currency exchanges are allowed (No monetary payments)
2. Tangible items to trade can include: books, textbooks, gear, calculators, chargers, etc.
3. Intangible items to trade can include: tutoring, coaching, photoshoots, digital goods, etc.
4. Your .EDU email is used for others to contact you to barter
5. We do NOT inspect items, guarantee trades, or assume liability for peer swaps. Please practice common sense and meet in safe, public campus locations for all exchanges.
