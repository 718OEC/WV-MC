Barter Bazaar

Barter Rules:
  • No currencies; only bartering is allowed.
  • No illegal or illicit items/services can be bartered.
  • Trades can include: books, textbooks, gear, calculators, chargers, tutoring, coaching, photoshoots, digital goods, etc.
  • Your school email is used for intiating contact
  • We do not inspect items, guarantee trades, or assume liability for peer swaps. Practice safety for all exchanges.
